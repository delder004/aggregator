# 02_people_market

Job: People and market.

Read the [root context](../CONTEXT.md). Write results under `<run>/02_people_market/`.

## Inputs

Stage 01 and growth CLI documentation; prior dated evidence where relevant.

## Process

Use the growth pipeline to establish current discovery, queries, traffic and measurement limits. Reuse suitable evidence with dates and scopes; never present a reused observation as a fresh measurement. Explore materially different people and jobs (normally three or four), their triggering situations, current workarounds, desired outcomes and reasons to return. Distinguish observed behavior, public descriptions and inference; desk research is not an interview.

Investigate demand, changes in the market, competitors and substitutes at the task level. Inspect at least five relevant primary sources/products across the explored jobs and search for disconfirming evidence. Include competitor experience and editorial choices, not just feature presence. Ask what is well served, frustrating, fragmented, or becoming possible. Existing competition can establish demand or reveal a different approach; it does not automatically disqualify a concept. Record two intent clusters per serious opportunity and missing measurements honestly. Assess reachable distribution, without assuming access to others' audiences. End with strategic implications, not just an inventory.

## Outputs

- `output/research.md`: selected stage result.
- `handoff.md`: selected input/output links, judgment, unresolved issues and next stage.

## Human check

Continue authorized research without routine approval gates. Clearly distinguish user input from agent proposals; ask only when missing input materially blocks useful work.

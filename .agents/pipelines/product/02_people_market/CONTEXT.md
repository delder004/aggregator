# 02_people_market

Job: People and market.

Read the [root context](../CONTEXT.md). Write results under `<run>/02_people_market/`.

## Inputs

Stage 01 and growth CLI documentation; prior dated evidence where relevant.

## Process

Use the growth pipeline to establish current discovery, queries, traffic and measurement limits. Reuse suitable evidence with dates and scopes; never present a reused observation as a fresh measurement. Explore materially different people and jobs (normally three or four), their triggering situations, current workarounds, desired outcomes and reasons to return. Distinguish observed behavior, public descriptions and inference; desk research is not an interview.

Investigate demand, changes in the market, competitors and substitutes at the task level. Inspect at least five relevant primary sources/products across the explored jobs and search for disconfirming evidence. Include competitor experience and editorial choices, not just feature presence. Ask what is well served, frustrating, fragmented, or becoming possible. Existing competition can establish demand or reveal a different approach; it does not automatically disqualify a concept. Record two intent clusters per serious opportunity and missing measurements honestly. Assess reachable distribution, without assuming access to others' audiences. End with strategic implications, not just an inventory.

Follow the delta agenda with actual research. For the leading concept and its strongest challenger, inspect the best available existing answer to the same concrete task, rather than only their category landing pages. Record what the reader can already learn or accomplish, what remains unresolved, and whether our proposed interpretation adds anything. If access is limited, state exactly what was inspected.

Research the sources necessary for the concept's core promise. A cross-vendor promise needs at least two relevant product records inspected on the same workflow dimensions (scope, input, action, human review, eligibility, source date and uncertainty); do not infer parity from mismatched marketing terms. A recurring-change promise needs dated evidence of consequential change, not merely many announcements. If those cannot be found, narrow or challenge the promise. This is a desk-research coverage check, not product testing or a universal vendor quota for other concepts.

For acquisition, inspect concrete results/pages for a bounded set of relevant intent questions and one plausible distribution venue's public editorial/access rules. Record query, location/device when known, date and method; generic web search is not a controlled ranking measurement. Evaluate the kind of answer winning pages provide and a specific contribution we could make. Do not claim audience access or demand volume from public availability. Do not buy API requests to satisfy a checklist.

## Outputs

- `output/research.md`: selected stage result.
- `handoff.md`: selected input/output links, judgment, unresolved issues and next stage.

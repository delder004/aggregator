# 03_strategy

Job: Strategic theses.

Read the [root context](../CONTEXT.md). Write results under `<run>/03_strategy/`.

## Inputs

Stages 01–02.

## Process

Develop three distinct strategic bets. Each needs a specific audience and job, belief about change, reason now, reason we could win, promise, willingness to sacrifice other opportunities, and an advantage that could compound. A collection of SEO pages is not a strategy without a useful product behind it. Include the strongest argument for and against each bet, and distinguish evidence from conviction.

Explain how each could earn discovery, repeat use and recommendation. Consider alternatives to search-led growth. Identify what can be built or learned to create an advantage; do not limit options to capabilities we already possess. Keep a minimal-change option as an allocation alternative, not the automatic winner when evidence is sparse. Select candidates for concrete concept development; do not use a numerical score to substitute for judgment.

## Outputs

- `output/strategy.md`: selected stage result.
- `handoff.md`: selected input/output links, judgment, unresolved issues and next stage.

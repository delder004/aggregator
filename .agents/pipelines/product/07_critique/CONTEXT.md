# 07_critique

Job: Critique and experiments.

Read the [root context](../CONTEXT.md). Write results under `<run>/07_critique/`.

## Inputs

Final product proposal, earlier run, and review-rubric.md.

## Process

Review strategic quality and evidence integrity separately. If a subagent authored the run, the parent must independently read and critique the final before completion. Ask whether this is something worth building, whether its perspective is distinctive, whether the described experience makes that perspective real, and whether the first release preserves its essential value. A cautious, well-cited document can still fail. Challenge the recommendation from the strongest competing perspective and check evidence supporting its central claims.

Rank the few assumptions most capable of killing or changing the direction. Specify smallest tests, required access, practical continue/revise/stop criteria, effort and what each result would change. Proposed tests remain proposed. Put the resulting experiments in the final product document, not only this review. Compare with the previous run: cite specific changes in judgment and experience, not just added sections or a changed winner. Preserve substantive revisions in output/r2/ and update affected handoffs. Finish with an independent review and link to the selected final in stage 06. Mark unresolved failures explicitly; no claim of user agreement or product validation.

## Outputs

- `output/review.md`: selected stage result.
- `handoff.md`: selected input/output links, judgment, unresolved issues and next stage.

## Human check

Continue authorized research without routine approval gates. Clearly distinguish user input from agent proposals; ask only when missing input materially blocks useful work.

# 01_ambition

Job: Ambition and perspective.

Read the [root context](../CONTEXT.md). Write results under `<run>/01_ambition/`.

## Inputs

User request, prior product decisions, docs/design-roadmap.md, and known assets and constraints.

## Process

Recover the user's ambition and preferences before interpreting analytics. Separate explicit user beliefs, inherited proposals, and your own proposed perspective. Read the design roadmap as an input to challenge, not a binding product strategy. State what the site should make possible for people and a plausible long-term destination. Propose a clear market/product belief and explain why it is interesting; label it a hypothesis. Identify what we would deliberately refuse to optimize for.

Describe assets that could become an advantage and constraints actually supplied by the user. Unknown capacity remains unknown; hypothetical capacity scenarios must not silently become the goal. Ask a consequential preference question only if needed; otherwise make your proposed perspective visible and continue. End with questions for research that could strengthen, reshape or defeat the thesis. Do not lock in an audience or product before exploration.

For a repeat run, turn the previous independent review into a short delta agenda: prioritize at most three consequential gaps, state what evidence can be collected now, and what decision each could change. Preserve the user's objective and strategic ambition; a previous winning concept remains a hypothesis. State which evidence/outputs can be reused and why.

## Outputs

- `output/ambition.md`: selected stage result.
- `handoff.md`: selected input/output links, judgment, unresolved issues and next stage.

## Human check

Continue authorized research without routine approval gates. Clearly distinguish user input from agent proposals; ask only when missing input materially blocks useful work.

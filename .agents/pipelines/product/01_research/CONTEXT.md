# 01_research

Job: Research.

Read the [root context](../CONTEXT.md) and selected preceding handoffs. Write under `<run>/01_research/`.

## Inputs

User objective and authorization, previous cycle's decision if any, and preceding stage outputs.

## Process

Read the user's objective, product vision/preferences, existing design roadmap, previous decision and growth CLI documentation. Separate user choices from inherited proposals and agent perspective. On a new direction explore materially different audiences/jobs and substitutes. On later cycles research the specific unresolved question; do not regenerate broad strategy every time.

Establish actual traffic and demand evidence with dates, geography and limits. Inspect the strongest existing answer to the same task, not merely category pages. Research source coverage needed for the proposed experience; use compatible scopes and explicit unknowns. Reuse recent valid evidence rather than recollecting it for appearances. Public interest, keyword volume and competition are signals, not proof that users want our solution.

State the consequential uncertainty this cycle can resolve, existing evidence for/against it, and what remains inaccessible. Preserve strategic ambition and taste: what could be worth making, for whom, why now, and what we would deliberately leave out. Do not let a keyword choose the entire product. End with candidate hypotheses, not a full feature roadmap.

## Outputs

- `output/research.md`: stage deliverable(s).
- `handoff.md`: inputs, selected outputs, conclusion/status, unresolved issues and next action.

## Human check

Proceed with authorized work. Ask only for consequential missing input, access or authorization required for a concrete next action. A pending experiment must not be described as completed.

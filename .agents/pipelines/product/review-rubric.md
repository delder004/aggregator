# Review rubric — hypothesis loop v4

Review the product judgment and the evidence-to-decision inference. A well-written proposal is not a successful experiment. Use pass/partial/fail with specific consequences, not a blended numerical score.

| Check | Standard |
|---|---|
| Strategy and taste | A worthwhile audience promise, explicit perspective, concrete experience and deliberate choices. Existing vision considered, not blindly inherited. |
| Decisive hypothesis | One falsifiable claim with an observable response, scoped population and consequential investment decision. |
| Research quality | Best substitutes and counterevidence inspected; actual dates/scopes; no inferred demand from generic competition alone. |
| Test fit | The smallest credible test addresses the stated claim; demand can be tested before building. Interview enthusiasm, keywords and use behavior are not interchangeable. |
| Prior criteria | Outcomes, exposure, timing and accept/reject/inconclusive criteria recorded before execution; exploratory/retrospective findings labeled. |
| Actual execution | Observations traceable; unperformed work, access gaps, deviations and exclusions explicit. No invented users or simulated demand reported as real. |
| Decision integrity | Result compared to criteria; pending or inconclusive when warranted. Acceptance scoped to the tested claim, not proof of the whole product or authority to deploy. |
| Next investment | Accept/reject changes the next step. No automatic substantial build or full-strategy rewrite. |
| Repeatability | Hypothesis, protocol, results and decision recoverable in Markdown; next run links the prior decision; raw sensitive data stays private. |

For subagent runs the parent independently checks the decisive evidence and inference, recording review in stage04. An unperformed experiment must remain Pending even when its plan is excellent. Do not judge improvement by changed winners, longer documents or a more confident tone. Ask what was actually learned that changes the next investment.

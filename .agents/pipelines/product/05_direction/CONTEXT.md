# 05_direction

Job: Choose a direction.

Read the [root context](../CONTEXT.md). Write results under `<run>/05_direction/`.

## Inputs

Stages 01–04.

## Process

Make an argued recommendation for what the site should become, even when confidence is limited. Compare usefulness, distinctiveness, experiential quality, strategic coherence, growth mechanisms, compounding advantage, feasibility and evidence. Explain why the preferred direction is worth pursuing, what we give up, and why the strongest alternative loses. Do not choose solely because one concept has more existing keyword measurements or the smallest initial cost.

Separate three decisions: long-term vision, coherent first release, and smallest experiment. A test document cannot stand in for the product vision. Assess low and moderate capacity scenarios (2 and 8 hours/week only if actual capacity is unknown), changing sequence or scope where appropriate rather than automatically erasing ambition. Name critical assumptions and evidence that would reverse the direction. A recommendation is not a claim of validation. If no direction is defensible, explain the actual contradiction and still present the strongest conditional vision; do not end with an unranked list or generic call for interviews.

## Outputs

- `output/direction.md`: selected stage result.
- `handoff.md`: selected input/output links, judgment, unresolved issues and next stage.

## Human check

Continue authorized research without routine approval gates. Clearly distinguish user input from agent proposals; ask only when missing input materially blocks useful work.

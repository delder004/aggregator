## Claim and investment

Among 10 qualified accounting-firm Xero workflow decision-makers with a capture/approval change question from the last 90 days, at least 4 will submit a nonconfidential live question and select a follow-up slot after seeing the concrete manual-brief offer and freely available substitutes. This is qualified interest in a free manual service, not payment, usefulness, retention, search acquisition or market prevalence. Failure would stop preparation of a maintained reference until the problem or offer changes.

## Primary test: manual offer before building

Recruit 10 unique eligible adults who influence a firm's Xero intake or approval workflow, have personally encountered a relevant change/replacement question within 90 days, and can discuss it without client information. Do not count vendors, existing project collaborators, duplicate firms or hypothetical-only situations. Country, role, workflow and source used are recorded; claims apply only to this recruited cohort. A self-reported role is unverified unless otherwise documented. No assumed UK/general-market representativeness.

Recruit through owner-introduced professional contacts, subject to explicit authorization and actual access; neither currently exists. Do not infer messaging permission from permission to run research. Show identical offer and substitutes, without sales persuasion. No participant incentive. Explain the research purpose and optional participation. See ../materials.md for exact script. Plan effort: up to 14 days for recruitment after authorized recruitment begins, 10 qualified exposures, $0. Each confirmed exposure receives the full 7-day response window; final assessment occurs by day 21. If fewer than 10 exposures, Pending while still recruiting, then Inconclusive after recruitment closes and every exposed participant has had 7 days. A qualified exposure means participant confirms reading the offer and substitute links; log delivered, read and qualified separately so selective nonresponse remains visible.

Primary numerator: unique eligible readers who both submit a specific nonconfidential live question and voluntarily choose one of the offered follow-up times within 7 days of exposure. Denominator: all 10 qualified readers, including declines/nonresponders after confirmed exposure. Log each action separately; do not count compliments, clicks, hypothetical intent or consent alone. Accept at 4–10/10; reject at 0–1/10; 2–3/10 inconclusive. These practical allocation thresholds are not significance tests. Four manual requests justify servicing a small cohort, not building software; zero or one means little appetite even with a free offer in an intentionally problem-enriched group. If service capacity/available slots are not real, do not expose the offer. No invented calendar or promise of delivery. Review factual specimen before exposure and use it only as an illustrative format.

No sensitive documents, ledger access, personal financial details or private client data. Record participant IDs privately, publish aggregate findings only. Stop if consent is withdrawn or the brief would require regulated advice, confidential data or unsupported assertions. Do not silently remove a refusal from the denominator. If comprehension failures dominate, report separately and treat the test as inconclusive rather than redefine success.

## Authorized companion study: observed public problem signals

Purpose: assess whether public first-person accounts suggest the exact capture/approval boundary problem is worth recruiting around. This cannot accept/reject H4-01. Freeze six English searches, with no locale/device control:

1. `Xero Dext replace approval workflow problem`
2. `Xero smart document capture approval problem`
3. `site.reddit.com Xero Dext approval`
4. `site.accountingweb.co.uk Xero Dext replace`
5. `site.productideas.xero.com document capture approval`
6. `site.reddit.com Xero Hubdoc Dext switching`

For each search retain the first five distinct returned URLs in tool order (at most 30 raw slots); deduplicate across queries and report actual count. No query rewriting, extra-result cherry-picking or counting search hit totals. Open all potentially relevant user-discussion results among these slots. Qualifying report: visible first-person account of personally operating/influencing accounting workflow, specific capture-to-review/approval/publication difficulty or substitution decision, and date within October 17, 2024–October 17, 2026 inclusive. Count distinct threads once, require an actual account/question rather than a speculative product pitch. Exclude vendor authored advertising/help/SEO comparators, duplicate threads, undated/out-of-window reports and generic AI enthusiasm. Treat claimed roles as unverified. Search snippets cannot qualify a report without accessible source text. A primary user post on a vendor-hosted forum can qualify; a vendor reply cannot supply demand. Keep adjacent pain (e.g. extraction reliability, price) separate from exact boundary pain.

Outcome: number of independent qualifying exact-boundary threads in retrieved corpus, denominator all retained unique URLs; report accessible discussion-page denominator separately. At least 3 qualifying threads across at least 2 domains supports only a public problem signal worth recruiting on. At most 1 qualifies as failing this corpus threshold only if at least 15 eligible accessible discussion pages were inspected. Otherwise inconclusive. The high accessibility floor guards against treating poor search coverage as absent demand; the three-thread bar avoids building on an isolated anecdote. These rules are deliberately modest and nonstatistical. Two threads or a single-site cluster is inconclusive. Different people on one thread do not inflate the count. All findings remain public convenience-sample observations, not population prevalence or proof of desire for our brief.

Execution: six searches and bounded page opens, today, $0 paid-provider calls. Save query order, retained URLs, access status, inclusion/exclusion and date in corpus.md. Record errors without bypassing logins. No outreach/publication/site changes. Freeze this plan before new search results. Reused prior product/growth research is exploratory context, not fresh validation.

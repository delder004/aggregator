# H4-01: will a firm decision-maker request a workflow brief?

Claim type: qualified interest in a free manual offer. October 17, 2026 UTC. Status: untested.

**For accounting-firm decision influencers who encountered a live Xero capture/approval change question in the last 90 days, a concrete offer of a sourced one-question compatibility brief will cause at least 4 of 10 qualified exposed people to submit a nonconfidential live question and select a follow-up slot within 7 days.** We believe this because evaluating a tool feature separately from the handoff can miss the reason a process fails. That belief could be wrong: people may need implementation, accounting advice or vendor support rather than another reference.

Audience is intentionally problem-enriched; country is recorded and no geography-wide claim follows. The outcome is an action requiring a small commitment, not favorable feedback. No price is tested. One person per firm, no vendors or project collaborators. A negative result at 0–1/10 after adequate exposure would stop preparation of the reference; 2–3 is inconclusive. Four requests would justify fulfilling a small manual cohort, not building a site.

## The experience and judgment

**Bring one live Xero workflow question. Get a short, sourced compatibility brief.** For example: can changing capture tools preserve the review handoff? Lead with a scoped conclusion, compare input/review/approval/output, show source dates and name the one unresolved check. A copyable decision note is the output. Unknowns remain beside the affected claim. No fake product tests, broad winner labels or feed of generic AI stories.

The source-level insight from the prior specimen is that extraction overlap does not prove equivalent review/publication routing. The actual answer for a participant cannot be filled in before their situation and market/plan are known. The [prepared format and exact offer](../../03_experiment/output/materials.md) deliberately show unresolved fields rather than fabricate a case.

Best substitute: vendor help, BookkeeperTools' comparison, or the participant's adviser. The offer exposes free substitutes so requests reflect an interest beyond merely being told a new service exists. This is not a randomized preference comparison and cannot establish superiority. Strongest alternative direction: a buying desk when people primarily want a shortlist, or implementation help when they already know the answer. Log those reasons rather than force a compatibility narrative.

Vision: trusted maintained record of accounting workflow changes. Possible first release: one useful scoped reference. Current experiment: offer manual help using Markdown, with no site build. The vision should survive as a hypothesis while the specific first release can be rejected. The most consequential test is demand before polishing the specimen again.

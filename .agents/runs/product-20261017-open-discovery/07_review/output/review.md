## Decision

Accept the document as a **Proposed, conditional product decision and validation plan**. No blocking factual or scope issue found in the inspected material. It is not a validated product strategy. The preferred sequence is an explicit judgment under uncertainty; a different recommendation does not itself demonstrate better product-market fit.

The final document should remain in stage 06. The final handoff should select it and this review. No website, prototype, outreach, release, automatic publication or commit is authorized by this review.

## Findings

1. **Minor — readability.** Several numeric phrases lack spacing (`equal28-day`, `at least3-day`, `October17`). Normalize prose, dates and units before handoff. This is a formatting correction, not a new substantive round.
2. **Evidence limit — demand remains uneven.** New primary sources substantiate competing supply; they do not establish that buyers have unanswered questions or that a narrowly scoped answer attracts traffic. The decision appropriately retains low confidence and tests against existing resources. Keep this distinction when summarizing the result.
3. **Evidence limit — resource descriptions are not product trials.** CPA.com's build-vs-buy and practical-use-case pages were independently spot-checked and do describe relevant resources. Full downloads, courses and Atozai account functionality were not evaluated. The document labels those limits and does not claim the competitors deliver poor outcomes.
4. **Judgment limit — sequencing is not superiority.** Factual suitability research may be a more bounded test than implementation advice, but its actual upkeep cost is unmeasured and it may still need practitioner review. The draft says this explicitly. Retain that qualification rather than presenting C1 as a proven cheaper or higher-growth product.

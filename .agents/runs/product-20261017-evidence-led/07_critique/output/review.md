Yes, in a specific way. The prior run favored a recurring change desk on the strength of a single historical example. This run finds a strong direct comparison publisher and concedes that a table plus a timeline is insufficient. It makes the maintained workflow answer primary and treats change notes as support. That choice is better grounded than asserting a recurring news habit before seeing users return.

The central contribution is understandable: keep extraction, publishing status and external approval routing separate when examining overlapping software. The worked example now exposes actual route differences and exceptions rather than hiding behind a generic “verify with your vendor” disclaimer. However, the contribution is synthesis of accessible documentation. An assistant or knowledgeable reader may produce it without this site. The proposal is worth comparing against those alternatives; it has not yet earned a substantial product investment solely through desk research.

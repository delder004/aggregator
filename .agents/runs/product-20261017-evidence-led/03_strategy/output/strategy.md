# Three strategic bets

October 17, 2026 UTC · Proposed. Inputs: ambition and research.

## A. Workflow compatibility reference

For firm operations leads reconsidering one software handoff, explain which documented routes preserve the required input, review and output. Belief: increasingly overlapping AI features make boundary conditions more useful than product labels. Why now is supported by native capture's published expansion, not a forecast of AI adoption. Why us could become a disciplined, narrow evidence history and a recognizable way of explaining decisions; neither trust nor practitioner access exists yet.

Promise: arrive with a workflow question, leave with a scoped map and the exact unresolved dependency. Sacrifice broad news coverage, universal rankings and unsupported recommendations. Search can introduce a specific question; a copyable note can travel inside a firm; a meaningful revision can bring readers back. The accumulated asset is compatible records and the reasons decisions changed. The strongest objection is that good help docs plus a capable general assistant already supply this synthesis.

## B. Buying desk

For a firm owner choosing or renewing tools, make a clear shortlist based on constraints. Belief: independent fit advice matters more as product categories converge. Search intent and a finite purchase decision make the promise easy to understand. We could earn an advantage with practitioner-tested examples and maintained eligibility records, but the present corpus is not that advantage. Sacrifice general market tracking and learning.

Discovery through comparisons; sharing through a purchase memo; repeat use at renewal or a new client situation. BookkeeperTools already delivers a readable, segmented answer to the concrete Xero/Dext buying task. Atoz already adds editorial perspective. Our present evidence gives no reason to claim a superior broad buying desk. It remains the strongest alternative if real users bring selection tasks rather than handoff questions.

## C. Accounting AI practice lab

For junior staff and their managers, teach how to evaluate AI-assisted work through synthetic cases with explicit reasoning. Belief: demonstrated judgment becomes more valuable than tool familiarity. A useful case and rubric could earn recommendations from educators, search discovery and progression-based returns. The compounding asset is reviewed cases and observed error patterns collected with consent. Sacrifice news breadth and software coverage.

CPA.com and Microsoft provide substantial free substitutes. A case-based practice experience could differ, but we have neither qualified teaching capability nor evidence of audience access. Do not disguise a prompt library as a learning product.

## Choice to develop

Make all three tangible, favor A provisionally. Minimal change remains a capital-allocation alternative: maintain current pages and measurement while learning. It does not solve the user's ambition and is not a strategic vision. No numerical score can decide this. The consequential comparison is whether A's narrow handoff synthesis is worth maintaining compared with B's stronger existing answers and C's deeper teaching requirements.

## Task-level alternatives and experience

Public pages show what their publishers offer, not proof of adoption or effectiveness. Sources retrieved October 17, 2026.

- [Atoz methodology](https://atozaiaccounting.com/methodology), dated Sep 23, 2026: directory, editorial assessments and personalized watching/comparison. Strong counterexample to “curation is a gap.” Its structure is organized largely around vendors and rankings. Our proposed distinction must be a useful workflow decision that persists through changes, not another score. Its stated media relationship is an asset we do not possess.

- [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/transform-your-business-with-microsoft-ai/), undated: sequenced beginner business-leader learning path. Our practice concept would need authentic accounting feedback, not generic AI instruction. Course not completed.

We should not compete on general news volume or presume its audience is reachable. This is inspection of the publisher's own experience, not adoption evidence.
- [Xero July announcement](https://blog.xero.com/us/product-updates/smart-document-capture/), July 2, 2026, and [September update](https://blog.xero.com/product-updates/files-now-called-documents/), Sep 18, 2026: historical descriptions differ. The first uses Files and lists tax extraction as forthcoming; the second uses Documents and describes tax extraction among available capabilities. The second also locates Documents in the Accounting menu. These publications demonstrate a source-history delta, not current availability in a particular customer's account.

## Implications and disconfirmation

The Xero example provides a concrete specimen for a living workflow record: reconcile dated statements and show what the reader should re-check. It does not prove willingness to return, nor that this is hard to find without us. A buyer might only need the newest source. A firm might prefer its vendor/advisor to a new publisher. Atoz may already meet monitoring needs. These are decisive challenges.

Choose an experience around a recurring decision, rather than claim a vacant market. Broad bookkeeping interest is one reason to start near document capture and review, but no evidence says it has the best opportunity. Learning has a credible audience with a different growth loop; lack of measured keywords is not a rejection reason. The analyst audience risks reproducing the existing broad feed.

Acquisition we can actually control: publish useful indexable pages on the owned domain, offer RSS, make the answer easy to cite. Professional newsletter placements, practitioner communities, social account reach and recruitment remain unearned. No claim of distribution access follows from public availability of a channel.

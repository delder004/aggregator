## Judgment

The proposal is materially better product work than the previous run. It names an audience and a recurring decision, contributes a belief about the changing boundary between automated work and human review, and makes that belief visible in an example experience. The first release connects a maintained answer, dated evidence, briefing and team note. These are parts of one product, not unrelated traffic features.

I favor exploring this direction over the buying desk because its proposed historical record and return behavior reinforce each other. That is a strategic judgment about coherence, not evidence that the audience exists at useful scale. I would reject an execution consisting mainly of vendor summaries with a generic “verify in your account” footer. The editorial interpretation must earn its place by resolving a consequential question the original sources leave scattered or unclear.

# Direction: build the workflow change desk

Propose agenticaiccounting.com as the working reference for small-firm operations leads deciding what AI changes in bookkeeping work. Begin with document intake and review; expand into adjacent workflows only after the first record earns repeated use.

Choose this because discovery, user outcome and accumulated asset reinforce each other: a precise query lands on a maintained answer; a source change updates that answer; the reader brings a concise note to a team discussion; each edition adds useful history. This is a judgment about product coherence, not proven traffic superiority. The buyer desk is the strongest alternative and wins if users only arrive for one-off selection questions. The practice lab has a different, attractive learning loop but requires substantial teaching authority and is less connected to current assets.

Develop the roadmap's editorial data product idea; reject mention counts as the primary argument. Reject the prior run's substitution of “test a dossier” for a product vision. Preserve its fact discipline and useful decision artifact.

Destination: an independent record of how accounting workflows change, eventually with practitioner-reviewed firsthand evaluations. First release: a document-intake desk with one maintained workflow page, two dated brief editions, linked evidence histories for up to three relevant products selected by actual source coverage, an actionable team note, RSS and a visible correction method. It must contain completed, current material; a directory shell is not a release. Source-supported advice is about what to investigate, not operational deployment instructions.

Smallest experiment: show a complete static Markdown edition and its underlying record to five actual firm operations leads, compare to their ordinary information sources, and follow up after a second meaningful edition. It tests recurring usefulness before building presentation or personalization.

At hypothetical two hours/week, retain the destination but operate one monthly record; no weekly promise. At eight hours/week, attempt one fortnightly edition and maintenance, with distribution and learning included. These scenarios are planning sensitivities, not user-supplied limits. Initial release effort is separate. If expertise or maintenance cannot be secured, delay release instead of silently publishing unreviewed recommendations.

Reversal evidence: people need no repeated monitoring decision; the latest vendor article is equally useful; few material changes can be distinguished without costly hands-on access; intended readers cannot be reached; no editor can maintain the record. These change the product choice, not merely the button copy.

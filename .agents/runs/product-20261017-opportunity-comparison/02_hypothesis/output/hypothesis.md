# H5-01: a first useful AI accounting exercise
Version 1; October 17, 2026 UTC. Claim: initial use and follow-on interest.

For accounting students and people in their first three years of accounting/bookkeeping work who want to learn applied AI, a short source-checking exercise will produce at least five observable attempts and three completed reviews with requests for another exercise among the first 20 eligible recipients within 14 days of first delivery.

The offer is “Before buying an AI accounting course, try one real task.” It offers practice without a credential. The strategic belief is that learning to check an answer is a more durable reason to visit than consuming announcements. The specimen is [sample.md](../../03_experiment/output/sample.md).

Our strongest substitute is [Prompts for CPAs](https://promptsforcpas.com/), which already advertises exercises and worked examples. Our proposed advantage is a very small, approachable first task; this is not established uniqueness. Workflow guidance remains the challenger if this learning offer fails.

Vision: a useful accounting learning reference. Possible first release: a small series of reviewed tasks. Experiment: one Markdown task, with no platform, course, certificate or interviews. Failure should stop expansion of this offer, not trigger automatic redesign.

The earlier report proposed eligible visitors. This protocol uses eligible recipients with recorded delivery instead, because a Markdown message has no reliable view analytics. Criteria are frozen before any distribution. Delivery is not proof of reading; nonresponse remains in the denominator.

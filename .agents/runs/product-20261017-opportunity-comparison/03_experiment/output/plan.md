# H5-01 experiment plan
Version 1, frozen before exposure; October 17, 2026 UTC. Status: prepared, not run.

## Claim and investment

Test initial use and follow-on interest in a small practical-learning resource among accounting students and professionals with at most three years of accounting/bookkeeping experience who want applied AI learning. Passing justifies preparing a second exercise. It cannot establish SEO acquisition, learning efficacy, willingness to pay, retention or a winning market.

## Method

Use the exact [offer](offer.md) in the user's LinkedIn network. Interested people self-identify eligibility, then receive the entire [sample](sample.md) by message. No interviews, paid tools, website or contact enrichment required. Organic discovery includes second/third-degree connections. No randomized comparison: a single-arm feasibility pilot.

The strongest substitute already offers practical learning; this pilot does not measure superiority over it. A later task comparison would be needed.

## Frozen outcomes

Primary cohort: first 20 distinct eligible recipients with recorded delivery. Start the 14-day clock on first delivery; stop primary enrollment at 20. Evaluate at day 14. Record later participants separately. If fewer than 20 delivered by day 14, classify inconclusive, not rejection.

Denominator D = eligible people delivered the complete resource, not impressions, comments or confirmed opens.
A = cohort recipients submitting a task-specific attempt (at least one source ID and an issue or corrected calculation); correctness is not required.
C = recipients with A who subsequently describe something corrected or confirmed after reviewing the worked answer.
R = recipients with C who explicitly request another exercise.
Count each person once per metric. Report A/D, C/D and R/D plus absolute counts. A like, generic praise or initial request does not qualify. Self-reported completion and answers that could be copied cannot establish independent skill improvement.

At D=20, accept initial-use hypothesis if A>=5 and R>=3. Reject this offer/audience/channel combination if either threshold fails, provided delivery and records are sound. Below D=20, missing observation records, a material content defect, or changed offer midway: inconclusive. Before the window closes: pending. These are practical continuation rules, not statistical significance claims.

20 recipients limit recruitment effort; five attempts prevent a single enthusiast driving expansion; three completed users requesting more justify only a second low-cost resource. The thresholds are investment judgments. Do not tune them after responses arrive.

## Operations and evidence

Owner: user account for authorized posting; agent can assist once specific publication/reply authorization and access exist. Budget $0. No paid subscriptions or incentives.

1. Preserve v1 files before publication.
2. Record post URL and time, exact approved copy, delivery times and resource version.
3. Maintain a private local recipient-to-ID map, separate from this repository. Use P001 etc. in the sanitized [ledger](ledger.md). Never commit names, profile URLs, message bodies or screenshots of private replies.
4. Record eligibility yes/no/unknown, delivery and A/C/R evidence timestamps. Unknown eligibility stays outside the primary cohort. Keep private evidence references for audit.
5. Capture aggregate denominators and exclusions at close. Nonresponse counts as no observed behavior; do not silently drop it.
6. Apply frozen rules. If a material error or privacy problem arises, pause distribution and version the protocol; do not merge cohorts.

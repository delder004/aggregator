# Search and forum opportunity comparison — research plan

Prepared October 17, 2026 UTC before new searches. User requests comparing opportunities in search and forum data before selecting a content-offer test. This is stage01 research, not an executed demand experiment. Incremental paid-data budget: $0; reuse available provider observations, no subscriptions or outreach.

Compare three candidate jobs equally:
- A: understand whether software changes preserve an accounting workflow's handoffs.
- B: select suitable AI bookkeeping/accounting software.
- C: learn and practice useful AI skills for accounting work.

For each inspect two commercial/answer-search queries and two forum queries, first five unique URLs in each returned tool result, then deduplicate across queries. Query order is tool order, not a controlled Google ranking. Locale/device are not controlled; do not invent location-specific demand from English searches. Follow useful links for depth but label them supplemental; do not use extra queries to inflate counts. Retain retrieval failures and duplicates. Sources accessed are primary documents for capabilities, or original forum posts for self-reported problems, not independent verification of identities.

Frozen query sets:
A search: `Xero smart document capture vs Dext`; `Xero invoice approval workflow integration`.
A forum: `site.reddit.com/r/xero invoice approval workflow problem`; `site.productideas.xero.com document capture approval`.
B search: `best AI bookkeeping software small accounting firm`; `AI bookkeeping software comparison QuickBooks`.
B forum: `site.reddit.com/r/Bookkeeping AI bookkeeping software recommendations`; `site.reddit.com/r/Accounting AI accounting software worth it`.
C search: `AI accounting practical training course`; `AI accounting exercises review outputs`.
C forum: `site.reddit.com/r/Accounting learn AI accounting course`; `site.reddit.com/r/Bookkeeping AI training practical`.

Forum coding: concrete first-person task/problem/selection/learning request; desired outcome and current workaround; willingness to try/pay only when explicit; provider pitches, anonymous identities, adjacent audiences and unclear dates labeled. Recent = Oct17 2024–Oct17 2026; undated/out-of-window findings separate. Count unique threads, not votes/comments or apparent people. Distinguish content-seeking from wanting software functionality. Multiple threads are not representative demand prevalence.

Compare problem specificity, fit for a content product, active information-seeking, strength of existing answer, attainable distribution, repeat/share hypothesis and source/reviewer maintenance. No arbitrary numerical composite. Reused search-volume evidence must retain geography/time window and missing values; broad volumes cannot choose a winner. Recommend one next content offer plus strongest challenger, or explain insufficient separation. Final output remains Markdown; no website, account signups, outreach or paid calls.

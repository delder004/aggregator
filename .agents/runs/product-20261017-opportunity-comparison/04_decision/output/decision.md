# H5-01: practical-learning pilot
Product pipeline v4. October 17, 2026 UTC. Decision: **Pending**. Experiment: **not run**.

## Decision and next action

The complete test is prepared, including a fictional six-row cash-summary exercise, worked answer, LinkedIn offer, response ledger and frozen protocol. No person has received it. Approval to publish the prepared post and respond to volunteers is the exact next action; browser/account access may then be needed.

## Hypothesis and strategy

Accounting students and people within their first three years of accounting/bookkeeping work who want applied AI learning will use a short practice resource even without a credential. Among 20 eligible recipients, at least five will submit an attempt and at least three will complete a review and request another exercise in 14 days.

The offer is “Before buying an AI accounting course, try one real task.” Users check six fictional movements against a deliberately flawed AI-generated draft, then inspect the worked answer. The editorial bet is an approachable first task with inspectable reasoning. The vision is useful learning content that earns return visits; this pilot is only one Markdown resource.

## Evidence and limits

The preceding [comparison](../../01_research/output/research.md) found explicit learning requests but also credential motivation and existing practical-learning competitors. [Prompts for CPAs](https://promptsforcpas.com/) already advertises exercises, so novelty and superiority are unproven. Workflow guidance remains the alternative.

The [protocol](../../03_experiment/output/plan.md) measures delivery, attempts, completed reviews and explicit requests separately. The denominator changed from proposed visitors to recorded recipients before exposure, because no reliable Markdown visit analytics exist. With 20 eligible deliveries, failing either behavioral threshold rejects this offer/audience/channel combination; fewer than 20 or compromised evidence is inconclusive. These are low-cost investment rules, not a significance test.

Actual D/A/C/R are all zero. Arithmetic checks establish internal specimen consistency only. Neither preparation nor agent judgment validates demand. No site was changed, no one contacted and no money spent.

## Next loop and review

If the pilot passes, prepare a second resource and test actual repeat use. If it fails with adequate exposure, revise the offer or investigate the workflow challenger. No redesign or course platform is justified yet. SEO acquisition needs a separate published-content test.

Quality check: claim and behavior match; alternative and counterevidence are visible; all thresholds precede exposure; user data remain private; no interviews or new tools are required. Review limitations: answers can be copied, the audience is self-selected, delivery does not prove reading, no control group, no independent learning assessment.

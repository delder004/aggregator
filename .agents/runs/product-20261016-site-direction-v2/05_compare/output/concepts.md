# Compare concepts before defining product

C1 sourced selection desk: firm owner shortlist; episodic, search/comparison acquisition; credible factual synthesis but Atoz/vendor guides already compete. Moderate upkeep, low audience validation. Strongest rival.

C2 workflow pilot field guide: controller/operations lead turns one bottleneck into a bounded test with reviewer and success/failure record; repeat with next workflow. Search how-to + peer sharing; quantitative demand unmeasured. Higher evidence-production burden, no practitioner access yet. Distinction must come from observed pilot learning, not a new checklist. Conditional leading hypothesis at8editorialhours/week with a qualified reviewer and recruiting access.

C3 market-change briefing: adviser/analyst decides what matters; recurringread, social/newsletter acquisition. High editorial burden and strong incumbent distribution; no owned audience proven. Defer absent channel advantage.

C4 verified AI accounting careers desk: professional finds qualified opportunities; episodic alerts/search acquisition;170head-term estimate, but AccountingflyalreadycoversAItrainer. High freshness/employer burden. Defer without employer/role-supply advantage.

C0 minimal-change: retain current URLs, correct obvious content issues later, fix measurement, run discovery only. Low-cost default at2hours/week; no claim that current feed is validated. No broad rebuild at either capacity until evidence gates.

Common dimensions covered in final: problem evidence, demand, distribution access, distinctive usefulness, repeat behavior, build effort, editorial load, confidence. Missing metrics do not count against C2/C3 as zero demand; their differentiation and channel access must be tested. Capacity chooses C0 at2hours; at8hours choose C2 validation versus C1, not production rollout. Reverse to C1 if practitioners can shortlist more effectively with sources but cannot use pilot plans, or reviewer access cannot be secured; choose C0 if neither shows practical value or owner capacity absent.

Gate B: no concept validated, no implementation readiness claimed. Product document will make conditional choice concrete. No prototype or website produced.

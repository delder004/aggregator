Build your shortlist

Three questions before a demo.

01 / Fit

Does it fit this client?

Check the ledger, region, entities and transaction types. A broad integration logo is only the start.

02 / Control

What happens without you?

Ask what can be posted automatically, how exceptions surface, and how your team reviews or reverses a change.

03 / Cost

What will the pilot really cost?

Include client licenses, required tiers, onboarding, migration and your own review time. Compare the full workflow.
